## What this means
Under this V1.0 pilot protocol's nine scored variables and declared weights, Bridgeport's scored
building-form scenarios consistently produce a lower index value than Greenwich's single documented,
entirely single-family-exclusive RA-4 district.

## What this does NOT mean
- Not a townwide ranking of Bridgeport vs. Greenwich as municipalities.
- Not a claim about any other Greenwich district (only RA-4 has complete evidence).
- Not a causal claim about housing production or affordability.
- Not a complete zoning-restrictiveness measure -- V3 (height), V4 (site intensity), and V10
  (floor-area capacity) are excluded from the numeric aggregate (see KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md).
- Not a legal opinion.
